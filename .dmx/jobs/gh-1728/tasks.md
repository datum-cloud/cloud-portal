## Phase 1: o11y-metrics service
- [x] Add `app/resources/o11y-metrics/o11y-metrics.service.ts` with `createO11yMetricsService({ client? })`, modeled on `o11y-log.service.ts`. `query` and `queryRange` are form-encoded POSTs to `/apis/o11y.miloapis.com/v1alpha1/metrics/api/v1/{query,query_range}`. `labelValues(label, match?)` is a GET to `/label/{label}/values` with `match[]`.
